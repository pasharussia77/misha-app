## 2. Content process and English text

- Before building any new unit, show Galina the words, sentences and the FULL-length story (and role-plays) for review first; build only after approval.
- Content method: combine Claude-written sentences with native-speaker sentences from Tatoeba (and word-frequency lists to choose useful words); check self-written sentences against native usage; mark each sentence's source (Tatoeba / Claude) in reviews. For languages Galina can't check (e.g. Spanish), lean more on native sentences. (If Tatoeba is unreachable from the sandbox, say so in the review page and mark every sentence C.)
- When building units, follow the doc "Миша: content resource library" (claude.ai/artifact/N9fuWh59XzCVxaWucn5TyJ): its sources, method and credit rules.
- Never shorten a story. Content must fit the unit topic.
- Check new text programmatically against all vocabulary taught so far (units 1 to current) before showing it.
- Russian register: do not mix polite «вы» and informal «ты» in one exchange (Здравствуйте goes with вы, Здравствуй with ты). Children say «Здравствуйте» and «Извините» to a teacher; a teacher says «Здравствуй» and «ты» to a child. Do not describe a family member as old, do not answer a compliment with the same adjective («ты добрая»), say «Спасибо». «Вкусно» is for food, not a place. With a plane use «лететь», not «ехать». Use «мои» (no stress mark) for plural «my».

ENGLISH TEXT RULE (Galina Nov 26 2026): all English in the app and in review pages (word glosses, sentence translations, story/chat lines, questions, hints, tips) = natural AMERICAN English for American kids, written for MEANING, not a literal word-for-word gloss of the Russian/Spanish (also Spanish→English). Write the English the way a US child would say it. US words: store (not shop), math, movies/movie theater (not cinema), recess (not break), fall (not autumn), rocks (not stones), Mom/Dad, soccer, TV, bike, "at home". Russian «был/была + place» = went/visited ("Mom went to the store"), static place = "at the store/museum/library/café"; «гулять» = "went for a walk"; «Сегодня математика» = "We have math today"; «Я иду» = on foot, say "walking" when it matters. Mark gender/role differences in glosses (teacher (man)/(woman)). Before showing any text to Galina, do an English self-check pass (US vocabulary, meaning, natural phrasing, polite ты/вы consistency) and fix, so she never has to.

Note: units 1-20 English was never audited against this rule (offered to Galina Nov 26 2026, no decision yet).

### 4.6 Pointing and checking lines
Mishaling voice rule, "pointing and checking" lines (Galina approved Nov 11 2026): when a character picks up/points at a thing and asks if it is X (e.g. Misha after Tima corrects him: "Ой! А э́то? Я́блоко?"), the TTS reads "А это яблоко?" as stress only on the noun or contrastive. Fix = write it as TWO short questions so the pointing word gets its own stress and a natural pause: "Ой! А э́то? Я́блоко?" (mark on э́то and on я́блоко, generated in ONE request; stitching two clips with silence sounded bad). Avoid priming contrast: lines like "Это не X! Это Y!" just before push the model to stress это contrastively. Capitals, dashes, ellipses, commas did not give the right stress. When a line has a wrong stress, make 3-4 variants and let Galina pick; only fix lines with wrong accent/stress, teacher lines are fine otherwise.

### 4.7 End-of-clip noise, drift, t/d check
Mishaling voices, END-OF-CLIP NOISE rule (found Nov 11 2026): NEVER send next_text (or next_request_ids) to ElevenLabs for story lines; it makes the voice start the first word of the next line at the end of the clip (silence, then a short burst of a new word, cut off). Use previous_text only (still gives natural stress). Every new clip must pass a tail check before use: after normalizing, find speech segments (frames above about -45 dB relative to peak, gaps of 0.12 s or more); fail if the last segment is short (<0.35 s) and isolated (gap of 0.2 s or more) or the clip runs more than about 0.25 s after the last word. If a clip fails, the cheapest fix is a local trim (cut at last real speech end + 0.12 s, 60 ms fade-out), regenerate without next_text only if trimming would cut real speech.

Mishaling voices, ElevenLabs-side drift (found Nov 11 2026, not our bug): occasionally a Misha clip (Lunya, stability 0.35) comes out in another-sounding voice (first line of a story, no previous_text) or with a wrong first consonant (ты→ды, три→дри). Requests were correct (voice id/settings logged), so only fix = regenerate that single clip with a different seed (base seed + n*7919), 2 takes, pick the one most similar to the average Misha LTAS, update the same listening artifact; log seeds/request ids. Tiny cost (~20 credits per 3 lines). Galina notes such lines while listening unit by unit.

Mishaling voices, T vs D start-of-clip check (found Nov 11 2026): ElevenLabs sometimes says ты/три with "д". Free local detector: in the first 0.5 s, find the burst (first frame where >2.5 kHz energy exceeds 25% of its max within the first 200 ms) and measure low-band (<500 Hz) energy in the 40 ms before it relative to the clip's low-band peak: "т" ≈ 0.003-0.007, "д" ≈ 0.2-0.5. Use it to test candidates before sending to Galina; generate several takes with different seeds (base + n*104729) and previous_text variants (for a story's first line an artificial previous line = last Misha line of the previous story), keep the first take that passes; stability changes did not help. Voice-identity differences ("not Misha's voice") are NOT measurable by simple spectrum/f0 checks; only Galina's ear decides, so give a different take and let her check.
